What is the General Term of a Sequence?

A sequence is a list of numbers written in order. We call them:

  • T1 → the 1st term
  • T2 → the 2nd term
  • T3 → the 3rd term
  • … and Tn → the nth term

Example: Even numbers 2, 4, 6, 8, …

T12
T24
T36
T48
…

Look at the pattern:

T1 = 2 × 1
T2 = 2 × 2
T3 = 2 × 3
T4 = 2 × 4

So the general term is:

Tn = 2n

Once you have the general term, you can find any term without listing the whole sequence!

Try it: the 100th term is T100 = 2 × 100 = 200.

⚙️ The Number Machine

Think of the general term as a machine. You put in a number n (the position) and it spits out the term Tn.
